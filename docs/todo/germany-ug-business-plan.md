## 1. Why UG (haftungsbeschränkt) Is the Right Structure

The UG is Germany's "mini-GmbH" — a full Kapitalgesellschaft with limited liability,  
founded with as little as €1 in share capital. For a 2-founder SaaS startup it is ideal:

| Factor | UG | GmbH | Einzelunternehmen |
|--------|-----|------|-------------------|
| Min. share capital | €1 | €25,000 | €0 |
| Limited liability | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Founders | 1–∞ | 1–∞ | 1 only |
| Formation cost | ~€600–1,200 | ~€1,500–2,500 | ~€50 |
| Formation time | 2–6 weeks | 4–8 weeks | Days |
| Upgrade path | → GmbH automatically | — | — |
| Tax treatment | Same as GmbH | Same as UG | Personal income tax |
| Perception | Professional | Most professional | Freelancer |

**Key rule:** The UG must retain 25% of annual profit as a mandatory reserve (Rücklage)  
until retained earnings reach €25,000 — at which point you can convert to a full GmbH at low cost.

## 4. Tax Framework for the UG

### 4.1 Corporate Taxes (Company Level)

As a Kapitalgesellschaft, the UG pays 15% Körperschaftsteuer plus 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag. On top of that comes Gewerbesteuer, which depends on the municipality's Hebesatz.

```
Tax                     Rate            Notes
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Körperschaftsteuer      15%             On company profit (Gewinn)
Solidaritätszuschlag    5.5% of KSt     = 0.825% of profit effectively
  → KSt + Soli combined: 15.825%

Gewerbesteuer           ~14–17%         Depends on city Hebesatz
  Formula: Profit × 3.5% (Steuermesszahl) × Hebesatz
  Example Hebesätze:
    Berlin:     410% → ~14.4% effective
    Munich:     490% → ~17.2% effective
    Hamburg:    470% → ~16.5% effective
    Small city: 380% → ~13.3% effective

  ⚠️ No Freibetrag (exemption) for UG/GmbH — tax from €1 of profit.
  (Individuals get €24,500 exemption — UG does not.)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL CORPORATE TAX (combined KSt + Soli + GewSt):
  Berlin:  ~30.2%
  Munich:  ~33.0%
  Hamburg: ~32.3%
  Small city: ~29.1%

Rule of thumb: Budget 30% of profit for corporate taxes.
```

The Körperschaftsteuer rate stays at 15% until 2027, and is then planned to be gradually reduced to 10% by 2032 under the investment stimulus law. This is a significant future benefit — by 2032 your corporate tax burden drops from ~30% to ~24–26%.

### 4.2 VAT (Umsatzsteuer)

```
Your service (SaaS / digital analytics):  19% USt standard rate

You MUST charge 19% VAT on all German client invoices.
For EU B2B clients (other companies): Reverse charge applies → no USt.
For non-EU clients (US, UK, etc.): No German VAT applicable.
For EU B2C clients (individuals): OSS scheme applies.

Practical:
  Monthly Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung via ELSTER
  VAT collected is not your money — set it aside immediately.
  You reclaim VAT (Vorsteuer) on business purchases (server hardware etc.)
  
  Hardware VAT reclaim example:
    €1,470 server × 19% = €279 VAT → you reclaim this
    Net server cost after VAT reclaim: €1,191
```

### 4.3 Dividend / Profit Distribution (Founder Level)

When you take profit OUT of the company:

```
Method 1 — Kapitalertragsteuer (Abgeltungsteuer)
  Rate: 25% + 5.5% Soli + possibly Kirchensteuer (~8-9%)
  Effective rate: ~26.4–28%
  Applied to: Dividends/Gewinnausschüttungen
  Example: €50,000 profit distributed → ~€13,200–14,000 in personal tax

Method 2 — Teileinkünfteverfahren (more complex, often better for high earners)
  Applied to: If you are also Geschäftsführer
  60% of dividend taxed at personal income tax rate
  40% is tax-free
  Better if your personal income tax rate < 42%

Method 3 — Geschäftsführergehalt (BEST for tax optimization)
  Pay yourselves a salary from the UG
  Salary = company Betriebsausgabe → reduces company profit → reduces KSt + GewSt
  Salary taxed at personal Einkommensteuer rates
  ⚠️ But: triggers Sozialversicherung (health, pension, unemployment insurance)
  
  Sozialversicherung split (employer + employee):
    Health insurance (KV):      ~14.6% (split ~50/50) 
    Nursing care (PV):          ~3.4% (split ~50/50)
    Pension (RV):               ~18.6% (split ~50/50)
    Unemployment (AV):          ~2.6% (split ~50/50)
    Total:                      ~39.2% of gross salary
    Employer half:              ~19.6% (= extra cost to UG)
    Employee half:              ~19.6% (= deducted from your salary)
    
  Salary cap for pension/unemployment SV:
    Contribution ceiling 2025:  €7,550/month (West), €7,450 (East)
    Above this ceiling: no more SV contributions
```

## 8. VAT Cash Flow Warning

This is a common trap for German founders:

```
Example: Client pays €19/month subscription
  Net (yours):    €15.97
  VAT (19%):      €3.03 → belongs to Finanzamt, NOT to you

At 1,000 clients × €19 gross:
  Monthly revenue (gross): €19,000
  Monthly VAT liability:   €3,025
  Your actual revenue:     €15,975

⚠️ Always keep VAT in a separate account.
   File Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung monthly via ELSTER.
   
For international clients (B2B EU, non-EU):
  No German VAT — but check OSS (One Stop Shop) for EU B2C.
  International SaaS revenue should be the majority →
  reduces your VAT headache significantly.
```

## 9. UG → GmbH Upgrade Path

```
Trigger: When retained reserves (Rücklage) reach €25,000.

At €200,000+ revenue: this happens in Year 2–3.

Upgrade process:
  - Capital increase resolution (Gesellschafterbeschluss)
  - Notary + Handelsregister amendment: ~€800–1,500
  - Changes the legal suffix from "UG (haftungsbeschränkt)" to "GmbH"
  - No tax event, no restructuring needed
  - Credibility improvement with enterprise clients

Recommendation: Plan to convert by Year 3 when you have 500+ clients.
```
