**Title:** One Number. The Whole Picture.  
**Body:** The Global Alignment Score combines four independent layers — technical momentum, news sentiment, macro economics, and volatility regime — into a single 0–100 score. A score above 60 means tailwinds. Below 40 means headwinds. And the breakdown tells you exactly why.

### Market Weather System
**Title:** Market Conditions in Plain English.  
**Body:** "Strong Tailwind." "Mixed Signals." "High Instability." The Market Weather System translates complex signal aggregation into language any investor can act on. No jargon. No ambiguity. Just context.

### Conflict Detector
**Title:** The Warning No Other Tool Gives You.  
**Body:** When technicals say Buy but macro says Sell — that's a conflict. Most dashboards hide these disagreements. Fin-Eye's Conflict Detector surfaces them directly, because divergence is often the most valuable signal of all.

### Technical Consensus
**Title:** 5 Timeframes. One Verdict.  
**Body:** Our ML models analyze 1-hour, 4-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly timeframes independently. The consensus across all five — weighted by each model's historical Sharpe ratio — gives you a risk-adjusted directional signal with no single-timeframe bias.

### Macro Dashboard
**Title:** The Economic Backdrop Your Trades Live In.  
**Body:** Fed funds rate. Yield curve shape. CPI trend. Unemployment. VIX. Recession probability. Fin-Eye pulls it all from FRED — the most trusted economic data source in the world — and synthesizes it into a single macro environment score.

### Backtesting
**Title:** Test Before You Trust.  
**Body:** Before you trade an idea, run it through 5 years of history. Fin-Eye shows you the Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, win rate, and equity curve of your strategy — plus an overfitting warning when backtest results look too good to be true.
