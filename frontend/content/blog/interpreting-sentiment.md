# Interpreting Sentiment Analysis

In the modern financial ecosystem, fundamentals and technicals are only two-thirds of the puzzle. The final piece is **Market Sentiment**—the collective psychology, mood, and narratives driving retail and institutional flows. 

## The Power of the Crowd

Algorithms famously parse central bank meeting notes milliseconds after they are released, but the crowd's reaction is often more powerful over days and weeks. Reddit boards, financial Twitter ("FinTwit"), and mainstream media headlines collectively shape the momentum of specific equities.

## Contrarian vs. Momentum

Sentiment data is rarely used as a simple "buy when happy, sell when sad" indicator. In fact, many successful traders use extreme sentiment as a *contrarian* signal.

### Extreme Euphoria
When sentiment scores are at absolute maximums—when every headline is bullish, and social media is filled with "to the moon" posts—it often signals a "crowded trade." Everyone who wanted to buy has already bought. Who is left to drive the price higher? This is often a sign of a market top.

### Extreme Despair
Conversely, when sentiment hits rock bottom and panic is ubiquitous, the asset might be heavily oversold. If the fundamental reality isn't actually as bad as the social media panic suggests, this divergence presents a massive buying opportunity.
